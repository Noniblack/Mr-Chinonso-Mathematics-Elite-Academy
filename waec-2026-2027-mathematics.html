WAEC Mathematics Examination Structure

PAPER 1

Objective

50 multiple-choice objective questions drawn from the common areas of the syllabus.

Duration: 1½ hours
Marks: 50

PAPER 2

Essay

13 essay questions in Sections A and B. Candidates answer 10 questions in all.

Duration: 2½ hours
Marks: 100

SECTION A

Compulsory Questions

Five compulsory questions of elementary nature carrying a total of 40 marks.

SECTION B

Extended Application

Eight questions of greater length and difficulty. Candidates answer five questions for 60 marks.

The 10 WAEC Mathematics Curriculum Areas

The published Mathematics syllabus presents 10 sections and 49 topics. The Academy will progressively develop learning resources around these areas.

01

Number and Numeration

Number bases, modular arithmetic, fractions, decimals and approximations, indices, logarithms, sequence and series, sets, logical reasoning, rational numbers, surds, matrices and determinants, ratio, proportion and rates, percentages, financial arithmetic and variation.

02

Algebraic Processes

Algebraic expressions, expansion, factorization, linear equations, simultaneous equations, change of subject, quadratic equations, graphs of linear and quadratic functions, inequalities, algebraic fractions, functions and relations.

03

Mensuration

Lengths, perimeters, areas, surface areas and volumes, including applications involving circles, sectors, similar figures and solids.

04

Plane Geometry

Angles, parallel lines, triangles, polygons, circles, constructions and loci.

Explore Plane Geometry →
05

Coordinate Geometry of Straight Lines

The concept of the x-y plane and coordinates of points on the x-y plane.

06

Trigonometry

Sine, cosine and tangent, trigonometric graphs, angles of elevation and depression, heights, distances and bearings.

07

Introductory Calculus

Differentiation and integration of algebraic functions, gradients, rates of change and maximum and minimum applications.

08

Statistics and Probability

Frequency distributions, charts, averages, cumulative frequency, measures of dispersion and probability.

09

Vectors and Transformation

Vectors in a plane and transformations including reflection, rotation, translation and enlargement.

10

Units

Mathematical applications involving appropriate units and conversions.

Preparation Principles

Complete syllabus coverage matters. The published syllabus advises candidates to work through the full syllabus because examination questions are drawn across the topics.
  • Build understanding before speed.
  • Practise both objective and essay-style mathematical thinking.
  • Show clear working in multi-step problems.
  • Develop accuracy in calculations, graphs, tables and units.
  • Use revision to identify and repair weaknesses.
  • Practise under realistic examination conditions as readiness develops.
